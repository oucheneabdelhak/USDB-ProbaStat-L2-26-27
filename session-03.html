Position parameters

Mode · arithmetic, weighted, geometric, harmonic and quadratic means · median · quartiles

Today you will be able to:
  • locate the centre of a distribution with the right parameter;
  • choose among the four means depending on what is averaged;
  • compute the median and the quartiles, discrete and grouped.

Session 2 built the tables. Today we attach numbers to them.

1Why a position parameter?

A table or a histogram is still too much to compare, transmit and test. A position parameter answers one question: around which value is the distribution centred?

Two models, same mean
Model A: mean accuracy \(0.87\), standard deviation \(0.002\) — stable.
Model B: mean accuracy \(0.87\), standard deviation \(0.05\) — oscillates between \(0.81\) and \(0.93\).

Same mean, different engineering decision. Position alone is never enough.

One service, two summaries
Mean latency \(55\) ms, but 99th percentile \(220\) ms.

The mean describes the average user; the percentile describes the user who leaves. A report that gives only one is incomplete.

What a position parameter costs
A single number is a loss of information. The mean, median, mode and the four means of the next slides all answer “what is a typical value?” — but they answer it differently, and for some distributions their values diverge enormously. Choosing the parameter is part of the statistical work.

2The mode

Definition
The mode \(Mo\) is the modality (or the value, or the class) whose absolute frequency \(n_i\) is the largest. It may fail to be unique — when it is, it is called the dominant modality.
Discrete
GPUs per node: \(1\!:\!12,\;2\!:\!20,\;4\!:\!16,\;8\!:\!9,\;16\!:\!3\). Largest frequency \(20\) at value \(2\), so \(Mo=2\).
Bimodal
Platforms: Discord \(12\), Instagram \(12\), TikTok \(10\), X \(6\). Two modalities share the maximum: the mode is the set \(\{\)Discord, Instagram\(\}\).
Continuous — interpolation
Let \([a_i,b_i[\) be the modal class, of amplitude \(a=b_i-a_i\) and frequency \(n_i\). With neighbours \(n_{i-1},n_{i+1}\) and \(e_1=n_i-n_{i-1}\), \(e_2=n_i-n_{i+1}\): \[ Mo \;=\; a_i + \frac{e_1}{e_1+e_2}\,a . \]
Try it. Latency modal class \([50;60[\) (amplitude \(a=10\)). Change the neighbours:
Mo ≈ 54.87 ms
The mode is close to the centre of the modal class because the two neighbours are almost equal.
Use and weakness
The mode is the only position parameter available for a nominal character — computing a mean or a median requires additions or an order, neither of which exists on labels. It is the only one readable directly on a bar chart or histogram (the tallest bar). Its weakness: it is unstable; a small change in the sample can move it between two close modalities.

3The arithmetic mean

Definition
For modalities \(x_1,\dots,x_k\) observed with frequencies \(n_1,\dots,n_k\) on a sample of size \(n=\sum n_i\): \[ \bar X \;=\; \frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^{k} n_i x_i . \]
Simple example
Ten accuracies: \(0.865,\,0.871,\,0.869,\,0.874,\,0.870,\,0.868,\,0.872,\,0.869,\,0.873,\,0.869\). Here \(n_i=1\) for each, so \[ \bar X = \tfrac{1}{10}(8.700) = 0.870 . \]
Grouped example
GPUs \(1,2,4,8,16\) with frequencies \(12,20,16,9,3\): \[ \bar X = \tfrac{1}{60}\bigl(12\!\cdot\!1+20\!\cdot\!2+16\!\cdot\!4+9\!\cdot\!8+3\!\cdot\!16\bigr) \approx 3.93 . \]
Three properties used constantly
  1. Balance. \(\sum_i n_i(x_i-\bar X)=0\) — deviations cancel exactly.
  2. Linearity. If \(Y=aX+b\), then \(\bar Y=a\bar X+b\).
  3. Combination. Two disjoint samples of sizes \(n_1,n_2\) and means \(\bar X_1,\bar X_2\) combine to \( \bar X = (n_1\bar X_1 + n_2\bar X_2)/(n_1+n_2)\).
Sensitivity to outliers
The arithmetic mean is not robust: a single observation far from the rest displaces it. This is why service-level objectives are stated in percentiles, not in means.

4The weighted mean

Definition
For values \(x_i\) with positive weights \(w_i\): \[ \bar X_w \;=\; \frac{\sum_i w_i x_i}{\sum_i w_i}. \] When the weights are the absolute frequencies, \(\bar X_w\) coincides with the arithmetic mean.

Latency of a service
Endpoint /health: \(1000\) req/min at \(5\) ms. Endpoint /search: \(100\) req/min at \(200\) ms. Unweighted mean of the two latencies \(= 102.5\) ms, but the true mean latency over all requests is \((1000\cdot 5 + 100\cdot 200)/1100 \approx 22.7\) ms. The mean latency of a service is always weighted by its traffic.

5Geometric and harmonic means

Geometric mean
For strictly positive \(x_1,\dots,x_n\): \[ G = \bigl(x_1 x_2\cdots x_n\bigr)^{1/n} = \exp\!\Bigl(\tfrac{1}{n}\sum_i \ln x_i\Bigr). \]

The mean of products and ratios: speedups, growth rates, relative errors.

Harmonic mean
For strictly positive \(x_1,\dots,x_n\): \[ H = \frac{n}{\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^n 1/x_i}. \]

The mean of rates: throughput, F1 score, average speed.

6Quadratic mean and the chain of means

Quadratic mean (root mean square)
\[ Q \;=\; \sqrt{\frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^n x_i^2}. \]

The mean of squares: it penalises large values quadratically. It is the standard loss for regression (RMSE).

Chain of means
For strictly positive values, \[ H \;\le\; G \;\le\; \bar X \;\le\; Q , \] with equality in each comparison if and only if all the values are equal.

Choosing the mean is choosing where in this chain the summary of the data sits — and this choice depends on the question.

7The median

Definition
For sorted observations \(x_{(1)}\le\dots\le x_{(n)}\): \[ Me = \begin{cases} x_{((n+1)/2)} & n \text{ odd},\\[3pt] \dfrac{x_{(n/2)}+x_{(n/2+1)}}{2} & n \text{ even}. \end{cases} \] It divides the sample into two halves: at most half the observations are below \(Me\), at most half above.

The mean doubles. The median moves by one rank, because it only depends on positions, not on values.

Median of a grouped variable — interpolation
Let \([a_i,b_i[\) be the median class (first class with \(n_i^{\nearrow}\ge n/2\)), of frequency \(n_i\), amplitude \(a\), and cumulative \(N_{i-1}\) of the previous class: \[ Me = a_i + \frac{n/2 - N_{i-1}}{n_i}\,a . \] For the latency table: \(n/2=50\), median class \([50;60[\), \(N_{i-1}=30\), \(n_i=41\), \(a=10\): \[ Me = 50 + \frac{50-30}{41}\times 10 \approx 54.88\ \text{ms}. \]

8Quartiles and interquartile range

Definition
The quartiles \(Q_1, Q_2, Q_3\) divide the sorted sample into four equal-sized parts. By construction \(Q_2 = Me\).
Continuous case — interpolation
With \([a_i,b_i[\) the class containing the fraction \(n/4\) (for \(Q_1\)) or \(3n/4\) (for \(Q_3\)): \[ Q_1 = a_i + \frac{n/4 - N_{i-1}}{n_i}\,a, \qquad Q_3 = a_i + \frac{3n/4 - N_{i-1}}{n_i}\,a . \] For the latency table:
  • \(Q_1\): class \([40;50[\), \(N_{i-1}=8\), \(n_i=22\) → \(Q_1 = 40 + \tfrac{17}{22}\times 10 \approx 47.73\) ms.
  • \(Q_3\): class \([60;70[\), \(N_{i-1}=71\), \(n_i=21\) → \(Q_3 = 60 + \tfrac{4}{21}\times 10 \approx 61.90\) ms.
Interquartile range
\[ IQR \;=\; Q_3 - Q_1 . \] For the latency data: \(IQR = 61.90 - 47.73 \approx 14.18\) ms — half of the requests lie in a band of about \(14\) ms.
Quartiles are percentiles
\(p_{25}\), \(p_{50}=Me\), \(p_{75}\). This is exactly why service-level objectives are stated as percentiles — \(p_{99}\) is the value below which \(99\,\%\) of requests are served — and not as a mean and a standard deviation. Quartiles are robust: outliers cannot move them more than one rank.

Common mistakes
  • Using the arithmetic mean for a rate — the average of \(10\) and \(1000\) req/s is \(19.8\) (harmonic), not \(505\).
  • Using the arithmetic mean for a ratio of ratios — the average of \(2\times\) and \(0.5\times\) speedups is \(1\) (geometric), not \(1.25\).
  • Forgetting to weight on an imbalanced dataset — the unweighted mean overweights the minority classes.
  • Comparing mean and median without checking the distribution — on a skewed distribution they diverge by large factors.
  • Computing a variance (next session) from a single observation — a variance needs at least two points.
